# Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Workforce Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, compensation, and attrition patterns using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The project combines **Power Query, data modelling, DAX measures, KPIs, and interactive Power BI visualizations** to help HR management understand workforce characteristics, identify attrition patterns, and support workforce planning and employee retention decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Organizations need a clear understanding of their workforce to make effective employee management and workforce planning decisions. Employee attrition can affect productivity, recruitment costs, team stability, and organizational performance. Analyzing employee information across departments, job roles, demographics, compensation, and work-related factors can be difficult when the information is available only as raw data.

This project develops an interactive **Workforce Analytics Dashboard in Power BI** to analyze employee characteristics and identify patterns related to attrition. The dashboard enables HR management to monitor workforce size, attrition levels, employee demographics, business travel, overtime, education, job roles, income, and employee tenure through KPIs, charts, and interactive slicers.

---

## 🏢 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall workforce size.
- Measure employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand workforce distribution across departments and job roles.
- Analyze attrition by gender and business travel.
- Examine overtime and attrition patterns.
- Analyze attrition across education fields.
- Understand employee tenure using years at company.
- Monitor average employee age and monthly income.
- Support HR workforce planning and retention decisions.

---

## 📌 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

The dataset contains employee-level HR information including:

- Employee Number
- Age
- Attrition
- Business Travel
- Daily Rate
- Department
- Distance From Home
- Education
- Education Field
- Gender
- Job Role
- Job Satisfaction
- Monthly Income
- OverTime
- Total Working Years
- Years At Company
- Years In Current Role
- Years Since Last Promotion
- Years With Current Manager
