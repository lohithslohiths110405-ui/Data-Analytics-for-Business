# HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze **employee demographics, workforce distribution, and employee attrition**.

The dashboard helps HR teams understand employee turnover and identify areas that may require attention for better **employee retention and workforce planning**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The HR department of a multinational organization wants to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, and attrition patterns. The objective is to create an interactive HR analytics dashboard in Power BI that provides meaningful insights into employee characteristics and attrition factors to support better HR decision-making.

## 📁 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle – IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance

The dataset contains employee information related to:

* Employee demographics
* Department
* Job role
* Gender
* Education
* Marital status
* Business travel
* Overtime
* Job satisfaction
* Job level
* Monthly income
* Years at company
* Attrition

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* **Power Query**
* **Microsoft Excel/CSV**
* **Data Visualization**
* **HR Analytics**

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### Page 1 – HR Analytics Dashboard

The first dashboard provides an overall view of the workforce and attrition.

**KPI Cards:**

* Total Employees
* Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Age
* Average Years at Company

**Visualizations:**

* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Job Role
* Attrition by Gender
* Attrition by Business Travel
* Attrition by Overtime
* Attrition by Education Field
* Attrition by Years at Company

**Slicers:**

* Department
* Business Travel
* OverTime
* Job Role
* Education Field

### Page 2 – Employee Insights Dashboard

The second dashboard provides more detailed employee and attrition analysis.

**Visualizations:**

* Attrition Rate vs Target
* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Marital Status
* Employee Details Table
* Attrition by Job Satisfaction
* Attrition by Job Level

## 🔍 Key Insights

* The overall **attrition rate is 16.12%**.
* There are **237 attrition cases** shown in the dashboard.
* **Research & Development** has the highest attrition count.
* **Laboratory Technicians** and **Sales Executives** show higher attrition among job roles.
* Employees working **overtime** have slightly higher attrition.
* Male employees account for a higher share of attrition cases.
* Attrition is particularly high among employees with **low years at the company**.
* **Life Sciences** has the highest attrition among the displayed education fields.
* The dashboard helps identify factors associated with employee turnover.

## 📈 Business Benefits

This dashboard helps HR management to:

* Monitor employee attrition.
* Identify high-attrition departments and job roles.
* Understand employee demographics.
* Analyze factors related to employee turnover.
* Improve employee retention strategies.
* Support workforce planning.
* Make data-driven HR decisions.
