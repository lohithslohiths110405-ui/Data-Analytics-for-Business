# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** developed to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a business dataset.

The main objective is to understand sales trends, product demand, customer contribution, payment preferences, and profitability. The dashboard provides meaningful business insights through KPI cards, charts, and interactive filters.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

You are a Data Analyst at a consulting firm. A client has provided a large business dataset and wants to understand key trends and patterns. Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover meaningful business insights and identify opportunities for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Understand product quantity and category contribution.
- Identify profitable and less-profitable periods.
- Compare sales performance across different states.
- Analyze customer-wise sales contribution.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Identify high-profit product sub-categories.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

### 1. Amount by State
Shows the sales amount generated by different states.

**Key Insight:**  
Maharashtra has the highest displayed sales amount, followed by Madhya Pradesh.

### 2. Quantity by Category
Shows the distribution of product quantity across categories.

**Key Insight:**
- Clothing – 63%
- Electronics – 21%
- Furniture – 17%

Clothing is the dominant category based on quantity.

### 3. Profit by Month
Shows monthly profit performance.

**Key Insight:**  
November has the highest visible profit, while some months such as May, July, September and December show negative profit.

### 4. Amount by Customer Name
Shows the sales contribution of major customers.

**Key Insight:**  
Harivansh and Madhav are among the highest displayed customers by sales amount.

### 5. Quantity by Payment Mode
Shows product quantity according to payment method.

**Key Insight:**
- COD – 44%
- UPI – 21%
- Debit Card – 13%
- Credit Card – 12%
- EMI – 10%

COD is the most common payment mode in the displayed dashboard.

### 6. Profit by Sub-Category
Shows profit generated by different product sub-categories.

**Key Insight:**  
Printers have the highest displayed profit, followed by Bookcases.

## 🔍 Overall Insights

- Total sales amount is approximately **438K**.
- Total quantity sold is approximately **5,615 units**.
- Total profit is approximately **37K**.
- Clothing contributes the largest quantity share at approximately **63%**.
- COD is the most preferred payment mode at approximately **44%**.
- Maharashtra has the highest displayed sales amount.
- November shows the highest visible monthly profit.
- Several months show negative profit and require further investigation.
- Printers are the strongest displayed sub-category in terms of profit.
- High-value customers contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Sales and profitability vary across states, months, categories and sub-categories.

## 🛠️ Methodology

### Step 1: Data Collection
Collected an e-commerce business dataset containing sales transaction information.

### Step 2: Data Import
Imported the dataset into the dashboarding/Business Intelligence environment.

### Step 3: Data Cleaning
- Checked missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- Verified data types.
- Validated numerical and categorical fields.

### Step 4: Data Analysis
Analyzed:
- Sales amount
- Quantity
- Profit
- AOV
- State-wise sales
- Category-wise quantity
- Monthly profit
- Customer-wise sales
- Payment modes
- Sub-category profitability

### Step 5: Dashboard Development
Created KPI cards, charts and interactive filters to present the results in an easy-to-understand format.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Focus on high-performing states** such as Maharashtra and study the factors contributing to their stronger sales.

2. **Maintain sufficient inventory for Clothing**, as it contributes the largest share of quantity.

3. **Investigate negative-profit months** to identify issues related to discounts, pricing, product mix, shipping costs or other business factors.

4. **Promote high-profit sub-categories** such as Printers and Bookcases.

5. **Improve customer retention** by providing loyalty benefits and personalized offers to high-value customers.

6. **Continue supporting COD** while encouraging digital payment methods through convenient payment options and suitable incentives.
