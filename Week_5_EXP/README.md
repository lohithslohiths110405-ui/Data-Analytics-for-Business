

 1. Dashboard Overview

The Walmart Sales Dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales performance across different stores, dates, and holiday periods. It enables users to monitor sales trends, compare store performance, and identify business patterns through visualizations and slicers.

 2. Charts Used

* **KPI Cards** – Display Total Sales, Average Weekly Sales, Total Stores, and Holiday Weeks.
* **Line Chart** – Shows weekly sales trends over time, helping identify seasonal demand.
* **Clustered Column Chart** – Compares weekly sales across different stores.
* **Pie/Donut Chart** – Illustrates the proportion of Holiday vs. Non-Holiday weeks.
* **Bar Chart** – Displays the top-performing stores based on total weekly sales.

 3. Slicers Used

The dashboard includes interactive slicers to filter data efficiently:

* **Store** – Analyze sales for a specific Walmart store.
* **Date (Between)** – View sales within a selected date range.
* **Holiday Flag** – Compare sales during holiday and non-holiday periods.

---

 4. Key Insights

* Weekly sales fluctuate over time, indicating seasonal shopping trends.
* Holiday weeks generally generate higher sales than non-holiday weeks.
* A few stores contribute significantly more revenue than others.
* Sales performance varies across different periods, highlighting peak and off-peak weeks.
* Interactive filters help compare store performance and identify sales patterns quickly.

---

 5. Conclusion

The Walmart Sales Dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of retail performance. By combining KPI cards, charts, and slicers, it enables users to monitor sales trends, compare store performance, evaluate holiday impact, and support data-driven business decisions. The dashboard helps managers identify high-performing stores, understand seasonal demand, and make informed strategic decisions.
